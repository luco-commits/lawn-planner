Annual seasonal schedule

The schedule is organised as Spring Week 1 through Winter Week 13 so it can be reused every year. The calendar shows scheduled and actual dates. Weather moves unfinished work while retaining the weekly sequence; older incomplete work remains overdue until you record or move it. Mowing reduces during autumn and winter. Product quantities update automatically whenever the lawn size changes.
