ANNUAL SEASONAL LAWN PLANNER FOR ANDROID

Upload all five files in this folder to the root of the same GitHub Pages repository.

Core product stock starts full at the beginning of the cycle. Product use is calculated behind the scenes from completed product tasks in the weekly schedule and the selected lawn area.

Product tile status:
- Normal: at least 30 percent remains
- Yellow: below 30 percent remains
- Red: less than one full application remains

The product card shows how many scheduled applications have been completed, but does not show an estimated remaining quantity.
